Verdict: software interrupts are modelled and processor exceptions are not, and `ltr` never populates TR -- it either runs against a table this probe could not verify (GDTR reads zero; #GP then #DF) or leaves a host-seeded TR untouched.  An IST stack switch is therefore not observable here: the kernel's TSS report stays at tr=not-loaded, and double-fault/NMI on an IST stack is an OVMF-or-hardware criterion.  Software-interrupt delivery is the control that shows the limit is the engine's exception path, not a mis-installed hook.
