  says the same about `no checks reported`.

  establish that a direct push to `main` would be accepted.

## 5. Verdict

**PR #36 is sound where it matters and should be left open for a merge decision.** No production
change, no evidence destruction, no manifest rewriting, no milestone-status change; its
verification claims reproduce; its disclosures (the 2 manifest drift failures, `no checks
reported`, the skipped=109 shape, the 55→51→55 manifest scoping slip) are the behaviour this
project asks for, and they were re-checked rather than taken on trust.

Two documentation defects were found, both in *claims about the diff* rather than in the diff:

1. **"§1–§7 stay byte-identical"** (PR body; the commit message's "§1-§7 are unedited"; design-doc
   §8's "§1–§7 above are unedited history") is not exactly true: two annotations were inserted
   inside/at the end of §6 and §7. Every original line is preserved; no line was edited or
   removed; but "byte-identical" describes a hash of the section, and the sections' hashes do
   change. **Minimal correction proposed** (a follow-up commit by #36's author, not a rewrite):
   replace the phrase with *"no line of §1–§7 was modified or removed; three blocks were appended
   — a re-framed header quoting the original status verbatim, a 7-line annotation at the close of
   §6, and §8"* and add the measuring command `diff <(git show 52f682c8:docs/engineering/USB-IDENTITY-BOUNDARY-DESIGN-v1.md) <(git show aed099d4:docs/engineering/USB-IDENTITY-BOUNDARY-DESIGN-v1.md)`.
   Reproduced here as measured: §1 33 lines/33 identical; §5 40/40 identical; §6 body 7 → 14;
   §7 region 40 → 86 (39 for §8 plus the annotation).
2. **"the four 'would have to be true' paths of BOOT_FEASIBILITY.md §3"** (record §8 of PR #36) —
   §3 lists **three** (`A.`, `B.`, `C.`; `grep -c '\*\*[A-F]\.'` → 3; `grep -n '\bD\.'` over the file
   → no match). Re-checked on the second pass of this task, and the finding is now sharper than
   "wrong number": **four is a real count in this repository — of `FIRMWARE_ANALYSIS.md` §5's ordered
   requirements — and nine is the count of `PS5_SYSTEM_SOFTWARE_TARGET.md` §4's classified
   requirements.** The sentence therefore conflated two documents rather than miscounting one, and the
   minimal correction is a **citation** fix: either "the four ordered requirements of
   `FIRMWARE_ANALYSIS.md` §5" or "the three paths of `BOOT_FEASIBILITY.md` §3". Changing "four" to
   "three" alone would produce a correct sentence that hides the reason the number existed.
   `BOOT_FEASIBILITY.md` itself is correct as written; the misattribution is only in the note about
   it. This is the second time a count of that section went wrong, which is why `M17`'s deliverable
   now carries all three decompositions with an explicit row-by-row mapping
   (`SECURITY_BOUNDARY.md` §3.1) rather than trusting a prose count at all.

Neither defect is material to safety, evidence integrity or the milestone graph; both are
material to the project's standard that a claim must be the size of its measurement. They are
reported here and in a review comment on PR #36, **not** fixed by editing that branch: this PR's
author cannot rewrite another PR's history on someone else's authority.

`docs/BUILDING.md`'s stale `107` skip count (§8.3 of the deliverable) is a third, pre-existing
defect in `main`, not attributable to PR #36.
